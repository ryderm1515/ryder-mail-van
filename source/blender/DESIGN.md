# Willow Lane cast: design language

One world, one hand. Original characters in the spirit of cozy Japanese
life-sims: simple, soft, every shape on purpose.

## Proportion guide

| | Total height | Head share | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Humans | ~1.35 m | ~40% (head ~0.52 m tall) | 2.5 heads tall. Compact bell-shaped body, stubby legs, mitten hands with a thumb nub. Eyes sit low (just under the head's equator), set wide. |
| Dog | ~0.55 m | ~55% | Head as wide as the body is long. Loaf body, four stubby legs, sculpted ears and tail. |
| Cat | ~0.32 m | ~50% | Sits upright; triangle ears; long curling tail. |
| Bird | ~0.12 m | ~45% | Round as a dumpling; tiny beak; short tail fan. |

## Shapes

- Built from purpose-made meshes: quad-sphere heads sculpted with falloff
  pulls (cheeks, jaw, skull), lathed garments from designed profiles
  (dress bell, puff sleeves, hat brim, boots), swept tubes for brows,
  mouths, straps, piping and tails, solidified shells for hair.
- No hard edges. Seams are read through piping and colour breaks, not
  creases.
- Silhouette test: every character must read from its black silhouette
  alone (hat brim + bell dress; floppy one-up one-down ears + curled tail).

## Faces

- Eyes are flattened lenses sunk into the head, so they read as part of
  the face, not beads stuck on. Warm near-black (#2f2422), glossy, with a
  big and a small highlight.
- Brows are thin tapered arcs; they carry the expression.
- Noses are tiny bumps; mouths are short swept lines (or a small open
  smile); cheeks are soft blush patches flush with the skin.

## Materials

- Matte, soft. Skin and fur roughness ~0.6 with a little sheen on fur and
  fabric. Only eyes and noses get a clear-coat gloss.
- Gentle roughness noise on fabric so it is not plastic.

## Palette

Warm cream #f3e9d2, sage #8fb08a, muted orange #e0915a, pastel blue
#8fb7cf, warm brown #6b4430, mustard #d9a441, terracotta #c9714f, blush
#f29c8c, skin #f0c4a0, ink #2f2422.

## Cast

- **Juniper** (neighbor). Cheerful early-riser who gardens before work.
  Wide-brim straw hat tipped back with a terracotta ribbon and a daisy,
  chunky bob with scalloped bangs that flip out at the ends, sage smock
  dress with a Peter Pan collar, cream apron with piping and a pocket,
  terracotta gardening gloves, mustard rain boots, pastel-blue watering can.
- **Biscuit** (dog). Caramel-and-cream pup who waits for the mail every
  morning. One ear up, one ear flopped, eyebrow dots, a peeking tongue,
  pastel-blue polka-dot bandana, white socks, curled fluffy tail.
- **Mochi** (cat). Calm, judgmental, grey-blue with a cream muzzle.
- **Pip** (bird). Bluebird with a peach chest.
